# European Electricity Prices and Energy Mix

An end-to-end analysis of European electricity prices, energy markets, and the 2021–2023 energy crisis.

## Tools

1. SQL
2. R
3. Git & Github

## Main Research Question

How are natural gas prices and electricity generation mixes associated with
non-household electricity prices across European countries, and did these
relationships change during the 2021–2023 energy crisis?

## Sub-questions

1. How did non-household electricity prices evolve across European countries from 2008 to 2025, particularly during the energy crisis?

2. How are differences in electricity generation mix — particularly natural gas, renewables, and nuclear power — associated with electricity prices?

3. Were countries with greater reliance on natural gas-fired electricity generation more exposed to electricity price increases during the 2021–2023 energy crisis?

## Data
Data are obtained from Eurostat.

- `nrg_pc_205`: Electricity prices for non-household consumers
- `nrg_pc_203`: Natural gas prices for non-household consumers
- `nrg_cb_pem`: Net electricity generation by type of fuel

The planned sample period is 2008-S1 to 2025-S2.
